Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept software review requests?

Yes! If you're experiencing problems with specific software and want to know about alternatives, we'd love to investigate. We prioritize reviews based on how many users are experiencing similar issues.

Are your reviews sponsored?

No. We maintain editorial independence and test software based on real user problems, not marketing partnerships. When we do include affiliate links, they're clearly marked and don't influence our recommendations.

Can you recommend software for my specific needs?

Absolutely! Send us details about your workflow, current frustrations, and requirements. We can often point you to better alternatives based on our extensive testing.
