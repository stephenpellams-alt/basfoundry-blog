Evernote Slow Syncing? Why Evernote Crashes and What to Use Instead

📅 April 13, 2026 ⏱️ 5 min read

Note-taking should be fast. Open app, type, save, done. Not a waiting game watching sync bars.

The Evernote Performance Crisis

Common User Complaints from Reddit:

"Evernote crashing on android - impossible to use" — r/Evernote, March 2024

"Evernote is pathetically slow, where to go next?" — r/Evernote

"IOS sync is slow. It takes me 15-20 seconds to sync just a few notes" — r/Evernote

The main problems:

  1. Slow sync — 15-30 second delays for simple note changes
  2. Startup crashes — App crashes immediately after updates
  3. Corrupted notes — Bad notes slow down the entire database
  4. Database bloat — Thousands of notes cause performance issues
  5. Mobile app lag — typing delays and UI freezes on phones

Why Evernote Has These Problems

  1. Legacy database structure — Evernote's database wasn't designed for thousands of notes
  2. Client-side sync — Sync happens on your device, not server-side, causing delays
  3. Heavy attachments — PDFs and images in notes increase sync time
  4. Cross-platform complexity — Syncing between desktop, mobile, and web causes conflicts
  5. Feature creep — Too many features added without performance optimization

Other Free Alternatives

Privacy-focused, stores notes locally, sync to your own cloud, completely free.

The Bottom Line

Evernote sync issues are frustrating, but better options exist:

For Office users: OneNote is free, syncs faster, and integrates with apps you're already using.

For minimalists: Google Keep or Apple Notes are fast, simple, and free.

For power users: Notion offers fast syncing, collaboration features, and a free personal plan.

For privacy: Joplin is open source, stores notes locally, and costs nothing.

Don't pay $120/year for slow syncing and crashes. OneNote, Notion, and other alternatives are faster and most are free.
