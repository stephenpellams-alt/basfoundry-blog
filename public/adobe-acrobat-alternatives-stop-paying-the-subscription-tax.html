Decision Framework: Should You Switch?

Definite Switch Candidates:

  • Annual Adobe cost exceeds $1,000 (3+ licenses)
  • Primary use is basic editing, commenting, form-filling
  • Team size under 25 people (simpler coordination)
  • Budget-conscious operations with cost control priorities
  • Frustrated with Adobe's pricing changes or policies

Consider Staying With Adobe If:

  • Heavy integration with Adobe Creative Suite (Illustrator, InDesign)
  • Clients specifically require Adobe-created PDFs with proprietary features
  • Advanced PDF accessibility compliance (Section 508, WCAG 2.1)

  • Team already highly trained and productive with Adobe workflow
